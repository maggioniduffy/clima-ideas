# F3.1 IMERG access research and probe

## Recommendation

Use **GES DISC Harmony/HOSS bbox + temporal subsetting on `GPM_3IMERGHH` V07 Final** as the canonical ingest, writing monthly regional Zarr pieces and validating time completeness before consolidation. The live Harmony capabilities response confirms bbox, time, variable subsetting and NetCDF-4 for this exact CMR collection. Final is the research product and keeps F3.1's intended provenance. Keep the last ~3.5–4 months on Late until Final arrives, then replace those timestamps with Final. The cloud-optimized dynamical.org Icechunk/Zarr mirror is the cheapest practical alternative when Late is acceptable, but it is a third-party repackaging and not Final.

Proposed bbox: **west −70.0°, south −40.0°, east −67.0°, north −37.5°** (30 × 25 = 750 0.1° cells). It covers the stated Confluencia/meseta envelope with a small grid-cell margin. Do not widen to the entire province: that would add mostly irrelevant area.

### Operational notes

- Do not download global source files. The 2000-06..2026-10 period contains **9,649 days / 463,152 half-hours**. The requested box is 750 of 6,480,000 global 0.1° cells (~0.012%).
- Harmony docs define `maxResults` as limiting input files; they do not give a collection-specific hard cap. Use bounded calendar-month jobs and monitor each job; numeric job cap and service throttle are **unverified**.
- `GPM_3IMERGHH` denotes Final. NASA recommends Final for research. Final latency is nominally ~3.5 months (often described as ~4 months); Late is nominally ~14 hours (recent observed latency may be closer to 12 hours). Final incorporates monthly gauge adjustment/revision; Late is low-latency and can differ. Use Late only as a provisional recent tail, and replace it with Final when available.

## Probe (2014-04-07)

Source: dynamical.org NASA IMERG analysis, Late V07, accessed via public Icechunk Zarr. Requested bbox: −70..−67° lon, −40..−37.5° lat; selected cell centers cover −69.95..−67.05° and −39.95..−37.55°. This directionally selects 30 × 25 grid centers. The local NetCDF contains 48 half-hours, **316,000 bytes**, and two variables: `precipitation_surface` and `precipitation_quality_index_surface`. Wall time, including load and local write: **8.59 s**. Network bytes were not measured; the documented chunk size is only a transfer planning proxy.

Grid spacing: **0.1° × 0.1°**. `precipitation_surface` units are **kg m⁻² s⁻¹** (equivalent numerically to mm/s); converted to mm/h by ×3600 and daily depth by summing each half-hour rate ×1800 s. Nearest cell to Neuquén Aero (−38.95, −68.16) is (−38.95, −68.15): **daily max 30-min mean rate 10.712 mm/h; daily sum 59.389 mm**. This is the Late mirror's value, not a Final validation.

The requested domain estimate is 316,000 bytes/day × 9,649 = **3,049,084,000 bytes (~3.05 GB decimal)** for this probe's NetCDF variables/encoding. This is not an exact Zarr estimate: variable set, encoding, write chunking, compression and any missing-value conventions will change it. The catalog's 54.9 MiB source chunk size implies monthly chunk reads can transfer much more than the small regional output. Benchmark a full month before committing to archive-scale runtime/storage.

Retrying the same request with `harmony.Client(token=earthaccess.get_edl_token())` returned HTTP 401 from Earthdata OAuth `/oauth/authorize`. ```python

    collection,

## Final month benchmark

- Run date: 2026-11-16. Reached `harmony.earthdata.nasa.gov` and `urs.earthdata.nasa.gov` (HTTP 200). Credentials were read only from the environment and never printed or persisted.
- Variable subset `Grid/precipitation`: 2 granules, 94,762 bytes total (47,381 bytes/granule). Existing all-variable comparison: 214,723 bytes for the same two granules; subset reduced bytes by 55.9%.
- April job: one submission, 1,440 input granules, `maxResults` omitted. Submit-to-success wall time was 138.21 s. Harmony auto-paused at 13% with 80 preview outputs; one explicit resume completed the same job. No job failure.
- Downloads: `NUM_REQUESTS_WORKERS=4`; Harmony output page limit is at most 2,000 links and this result used one page of 1,440. The first 80 preview files downloaded in 46.59 s; the final `download_all` pass completed 1,440 futures in 660.52 s, skipping those cached 80. 1,440 files, 66,031,863 raw bytes; zero download errors and no manual retries.
- Combined into `final-201404.zarr`: 1,440 unique, sorted, complete 30-minute timestamps (2014-04-01 00:00 through 2014-04-30 23:30 UTC). Zarr file payload is 355,025 bytes (directory apparent size 477,905 bytes). All April raw `.nc4` files were deleted after validation.
- Zarr cell nearest (-38.95, -68.16) is (-38.95, -68.15). On 2014-04-07, Final daily total is **92.015 mm** and maximum 30-minute rate is **15.410 mm/h**; Late mirror values were 59.389 mm and 10.712 mm/h (Final higher by 32.626 mm and 4.698 mm/h).
- Extrapolation for 317 months, assuming the measured per-month raw/Zarr sizes and independent, linear jobs: **20,932,100,571 raw bytes (~20.93 GB)** and **112,542,925 Zarr bytes (~112.54 MB)**. At 138.21 s processing plus 660.52 s final download per month: sequential **70.33 h**; two concurrent jobs, ideal no-contention **35.17 h**. These timings do not include consolidation overhead.
- Capabilities report `concatenation: false`. `maxResults` is the optional input-granule cap; this request left it unset and processed all 1,440 inputs. The result pagination limit is distinct: maximum 2,000 output links per page.
